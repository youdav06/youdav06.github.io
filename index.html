Borealga: spring-powered pickleball launcher

Team project, January to April 2026. Exhibited at Boston Children’s Museum Fun Labs.

We needed an interactive exhibit that launched a ball safely and consistently, then told kids right away whether they hit the target.

I designed a PETG plunger around a 30.42 lb compression spring, iterating until the launcher fired reliably every time. For the target, I built an IR break-beam sensor on a Raspberry Pi Pico, programmed in MicroPython, that lights an LED the moment the beam breaks.

Ferrite core inductance characterization

Research, W. M. Keck Laboratory for Integrated Ferroics, May 2026 to present.

The lab needed data on how nickel-zinc ferrite cores behave across frequency and coil geometry, to choose the lowest-loss setup for inductive assemblies.

I built the coil assemblies, validated field output using statistical error analysis, and swept each core from 10 kHz to 1 MHz. Plotting inductance against turns across more than 10 configurations confirmed the expected N² relationship, and I extracted core permeability from the linearized fit.
